Space Survivor — Privacy Policy

Short version

We don't collect your personal data. OrbitCrush has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no analytics of our own. Your game progress stays on your phone. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which processes some device data as described below. The one-time Founder Pack removes all ads.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress — ships, gear, research, resources, records, missions and settings — is saved in a file on your phone. It is not sent to us or to any server we run.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this file in your device backup. That backup belongs to your Google account; we have no access to it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner in the lobby, an occasional full-screen ad between runs and optional reward videos you choose to watch. Ads are never shown during a battle. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • the advertising ID of your device and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language);
  • interactions with ads (views, taps).

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy · Ad settings in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

Players with the Founder Pack see no ads, and the app does not start the ads SDK for them.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The Founder Pack is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; the app never sees your payment details. The app only receives the purchase status from Google Play to unlock the Founder Pack, and checks it with Google Play when the game starts.

In-game currencies (quanta, stardust, rescue beacons) are earned by playing and are never sold for money.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state — to load ads and to talk to Google Play.
  • Google Play billing — for the Founder Pack purchase.
  • Advertising ID — used by Google AdMob as described above.

The app does not access your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or precise location.

Children

OrbitCrush is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings deletes your game progress from the device. We don't hold any copy of it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date.
